The Blind Spot

Your integrations report success. Are they actually posting?

Most integration failures never trip an alarm. The API returns 200, the dashboard stays green, and the rejection hides in a response body nobody reads — until finance can't close the month.

The Silent Success

A transport-level HTTP 200 masks a payload-level rejection. The transfer "succeeded" while zero records posted downstream — no error, no alert, no trace.

The Reconciliation Gap

Records sent rarely equal records posted. Without a source-to-destination match on counts and dollars, a $48K variance sits invisible until a manual reconciliation weeks later.

The Audit Blind Spot

When auditors ask what actually happened on a given run, screenshots and re-pulled logs won't hold up. There's no tamper-proof record of the raw response at the moment of failure.

How It Works

From blind spot to verified posting

Three moves turn a green-but-broken integration into a defensible record of exactly what posted, what didn't, and why.

1

Deep Capture

We parse the full response body — SOAP faults, SeverityCodes, error items — not just the status line. The failures other tools stop short of are the ones we surface.

2

Three-Way Reconciliation

Every cycle is matched across source, integration bus, and destination — on record counts and dollar totals. Any drift is flagged the moment it happens, with the exact variance.
